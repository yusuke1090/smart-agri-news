## The risky time around calving

- The time from 3 weeks before to 3 weeks after calving is called the **transition period** (移行期, 周産期)
- To start making milk, the cow suddenly needs a lot of **energy** and **calcium**. But around calving she eats less, so she runs short
- Many diseases happen at this time, and one disease often leads to another (for example: milk fever → retained placenta → metritis, or ketosis → displaced abomasum)

## Main transition diseases

| Disease | Japanese | What happens | Signs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Milk fever (hypocalcemia) | 乳熱（低カルシウム血症） | Making colostrum uses up calcium very fast, and blood calcium falls too low. Most common within 3 days after calving | Cannot stand, cold ears, turns the neck to the side and looks dull. Despite the name, body temperature actually falls |
| Ketosis | ケトーシス | Lack of energy makes the body burn too much fat, and ketone bodies build up | Poor appetite (especially for concentrate), less milk, sweet-sour smell on the breath or milk, weight loss |
| Displaced abomasum (DA) | 第四胃変位 | The abomasum fills with gas and moves out of place (usually to the left) | Eats less, less milk. Tapping the belly makes a high "ping" like metal. Often needs surgery |
| Retained placenta | 胎盤停滞 | The placenta (afterbirth) does not come out within 12–24 hours after calving | The placenta hangs from the vulva. Leads to metritis |
| Metritis | 子宮炎 | Bacteria get into the uterus and cause inflammation | Bad-smelling discharge, fever. Delays the next pregnancy |
| Fatty liver | 脂肪肝 | Body fat builds up in the liver | Often happens together with ketosis |

### Prevention points

- Do not let dry cows get **too fat** (body condition score, BCS, 3.0–3.5)
- In the dry period, do not feed too much calcium and potassium (do not feed a lot of alfalfa to dry cows, Chapter 6). Anionic salts are sometimes used to help the calcium system work
- Keep cows **eating well** around calving (no sudden feed changes, a comfortable calving pen)
- After calving, check body temperature, appetite, milk yield and ketone bodies (simple milk or blood tests)

## Hoof and leg problems

- **Lameness** (跛行): walking with a limp or favoring a leg. It is a very common problem in dairy cows
- Pain means less time eating and lying down, less milk, and weaker heats

| Disease | Japanese | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sole ulcer | 蹄底潰瘍 | A wound on the bottom of the hoof. Caused by acidosis, hard floors and standing too long |
| White line disease | 白帯病 | The joint between the hoof wall and the sole cracks and bacteria get in |
| Digital dermatitis (DD) | 趾皮膚炎 | A red, strawberry-like sore on the skin at the back of the hoof. Spread by bacteria, especially on wet floors dirty with manure |
| Foot rot | 趾間腐爛 | The skin between the claws swells and smells bad |

- Prevention: **hoof trimming** (削蹄, about twice a year), footbaths (walking through disinfectant), dry and clean floors, comfortable stalls for lying, and feed that prevents acidosis
- Scoring how cows walk (locomotion score 1–5) finds problems early. There is also technology that scores it automatically with cameras

## Heat stress

- Cows are weak in hot weather, because rumen fermentation and milk production make a lot of heat inside the body
- When the **THI** (temperature-humidity index, 温湿度指数), calculated from temperature and humidity, goes above 68–72, cows start to be affected

![THI from temperature and humidity (heat stress guide)](images/thi.svg)

| Sign | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Fast breathing | 60 or more breaths a minute. Panting with the mouth open, drooling |
| Standing more | To cool down. Also bad for the hooves |
| Drinking a lot of water | |
| Eating less and giving less milk | Milk fat also tends to fall |
| Poorer breeding | Heats get weaker and conception rates fall |

What helps: fans, misting and sprinklers, shade, roof insulation, ventilation, plenty of fresh water, and feeding in the cooler hours.

## Other diseases

| Disease | Japanese | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bloat | 鼓脹症 | Gas builds up in the rumen and the left side of the belly swells. Fresh legumes (alfalfa, clover) can turn the gas into foam. The cow can suffocate — an emergency |
| Hardware disease | 創傷性第二胃炎 | Swallowed wire or nails stick into the reticulum. Prevented by giving the cow a magnet to swallow |
| Infectious diseases | 伝染病 | Foot-and-mouth disease, bovine viral diarrhea (BVD), enzootic bovine leukosis (bovine leukemia), Johne's disease and others. Some must be reported by law |

## Early warning signs

| Sign | Suspect |
| --- | --- |
| Eats less, ruminates less | The start of almost any disease |
| Sudden drop in milk | Mastitis, ketosis, displaced abomasum, fever |
| Fever (39.5 °C or more) | Mastitis, metritis, pneumonia |
| Lying down all the time, or standing all the time | Lameness, milk fever, heat |
| More activity | Heat (estrus) — not a disease |

Noticing "something is different from usual" early is what matters. Sensors watch for this every day, cow by cow, 24 hours a day, as numbers (Chapter 12).

## Summary

- In the 3 weeks before and after calving (the transition period), cows run short of energy and calcium, and many diseases happen
- Milk fever, ketosis, displaced abomasum, retained placenta and metritis tend to come together
- Lameness is common. Prevent it with hoof trimming, clean floors and comfortable stalls
- Cows are weak in heat. When THI goes above 68–72, use fans, misting and water
- Changes from normal in eating, rumination, milk yield and body temperature are early warning signs

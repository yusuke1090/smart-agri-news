# Appendix B. Key numbers

## How to use this appendix

- This table collects the numbers from all chapters in one place. They are rough guides for Holstein cows in Japan
- They change with each cow, farm, season and way of measuring. Use them as a first guess when you set sensor thresholds, and then adjust with real data

## Body

| Item | Typical value | Ch. |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Body weight (adult female) | 600–700 kg | 1 |
| Birth weight | 40–45 kg | 1 |
| Body temperature | 38.0–39.0 °C. 39.5 °C or more is a fever | 1 |
| Breathing rate | 20–40 breaths a minute. 60 or more in hot weather | 11 |
| Rumen size | 150–200 L | 2 |
| Rumen pH | 6–7. Long periods below 5.8 mean SARA | 2 |

# 0. How to use this book

## What you will learn

- What this book covers
- Which order to read the chapters in
- How to switch between Japanese and English

## About this book

This book covers the basics of dairy cows (mostly Holsteins): their body, breeding, feed, milk, diseases and farm work.
It aims to help you understand the words in the farming news on this site, and to think about **what to measure** when you watch cows with sensors.

| Part | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| Part 1: The cow's body | What a dairy cow is, the four stomachs and rumination |
| Part 2: Breeding and calves | Heat, insemination, pregnancy, "one calf a year", raising calves |
| Part 3: Feed | Roughage and concentrate, compound feed, timothy and alfalfa, silage |
| Part 4: Milk and health | How milk is made, milking, mastitis, blood in milk, diseases around calving |
| Part 5: The farm and smart dairy | A cow's day, barns, what sensors and robots can tell us |
| Appendix | Glossary (English and Japanese), key numbers |

## Reading order

| Reader | Order |
| --- | --- |
| New to cows | Chapters 1 → 2 → 3 → 5 → 8 → 9, then any chapter you like |
| Interested in diseases | Chapters 8 → 9 → 10 → 11 |
| Interested in feed | Chapters 2 → 5 → 6 → 7 |
| Working with sensors or IoT | Chapters 2 → 3 → 11 → 12 and Appendix B |
| Reading English news | Appendix A (glossary) and the English chapters |

## Switching languages

- Press **日本語 / English** at the top. The same chapter opens in the other language, at the same heading
- The English version uses plain, simple English. You can use it to practice reading farming news
- Select an English word or sentence and press "🔤 訳す" to see the Japanese meaning (you can also use the "🔤 訳す" button at the bottom right)
- Important words have the Japanese word after them, for example: mastitis（乳房炎）

## Using the page

| Action | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Press the chapter name at the top | The table of contents opens. Chapters are grouped by part. You can also jump to a heading in the current chapter |
| ◀ ▶ | Go to the previous or next chapter |
| "スマート農業News" at the top left | Go back to the news |

- When you come back, the page opens at the chapter and place where you stopped reading
- The address ends with the chapter number, like `#ch=9`. Copy it to send that chapter to someone

## Notes

> The numbers in this book are **rough guides** for Holstein cows in Japan. They change with each cow, farm, season and way of measuring.
> The disease chapters explain how diseases work. **A veterinarian decides the treatment.** If something seems wrong, talk to a vet early.

## Summary

- This book covers the body, breeding, feed, milk, health and farm life of dairy cows, in five parts and an appendix
- Switch between 日本語 and English to read the same place in either language. Use "🔤 訳す" for English words
- Numbers are rough guides. A vet decides how to treat a sick cow
